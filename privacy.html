Captionly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Captionly ("the app") adds automatic captions to videos on your Android device. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: your videos, audio and captions never leave your device.

1. What stays on your device

Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone using the open-source Whisper model. The videos you pick, their audio, the captions created, your edits, styles and projects are processed and stored only on your device. We do not upload them, and we have no servers that receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

The app uses the Android photo picker, so it only sees the videos you choose. It saves exported videos and subtitle files to your device's Movies and Download folders.

2. Speech model downloads

The first time you use a speech model, the app downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is a normal file download: no personal data, videos or audio are sent. Hugging Face, like any web server, receives your IP address when the file is downloaded.

3. Advertising

The free version shows a rewarded video ad when you export a video. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (for example impressions and taps) and diagnostic information about the SDK.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the app (menu → Ad privacy choices). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Captionly Pro users do not see ads.

4. Purchases

Captionly Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether you own Pro, and this is stored on your device so Pro works offline.

5. Data we collect ourselves

None. Captionly has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no servers of its own.

7. Children

Captionly is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

8. Security

All network connections (model downloads, ads, Google Play) use encrypted HTTPS.
