Umdah Crafts — A Reverse Clock: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Umdah Crafts — A Reverse Clock ("the app") is an analog clock widget for Android made by Umdah Crafts. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device.

1. What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal information.

  • The app has no internet access. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • The app requests no permissions at all.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Background photo

If you choose a photo for the clock background, you pick it with Android's system photo picker, so the app only sees the one photo you select. The app crops it and saves a copy in its private storage on your device so the clock and widgets can show it. Tap Remove in the app to delete that copy. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

If Android device backup is turned on, Android may include the saved photo in your own device backup. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account, not by Umdah Crafts.
